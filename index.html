FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Who can enroll at Kruthi Vikas Careers?

+

Any student in their final year, recent graduates (2020–2024 batch), or working professionals looking to upskill are welcome to enroll. We have courses for all levels — from beginners to advanced learners.

Do you provide 100% placement guarantee?

+

We provide 100% placement support — resume building, mock interviews, aptitude prep, and company referrals. While we cannot guarantee a specific offer, our track record of 500+ placements speaks for itself.

Are the courses offered online or offline?

+

We offer both online and offline (classroom) training modes. You can choose what works best for you. All sessions are also recorded for future reference.

What is the duration of the courses?

+

Course duration varies from 6 to 16 weeks depending on the program. We also offer weekend batches and fast-track crash courses for students with limited time.

What certifications do I receive after completing a course?

+

Upon successful completion and passing assessments, you receive a Kruthi Vikas Careers certificate. This is a digital, verifiable certificate that you can add to your LinkedIn profile and resume.

How do I register and what are the fees?

+

You can register by filling the contact form below or calling us at 9441602034. Fees vary by course and are structured to be affordable. We also offer EMI options. Contact us for a detailed fee structure.
